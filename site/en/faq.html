Mary Longo, services and costs

Mary Longo is a wedding and event planner and wedding celebrant based in Lecce, Puglia (Via Pistoia 8). She has been organising events for 25 years and weddings for 10, across Lecce, Salento and the rest of Puglia. She speaks Italian, English and French and works with Italian couples and with couples coming from abroad.

My wedding planning services start from around €1,200; the final fee depends on the type of service — from on-the-day coordination to full planning — and on the number of guests, the venue and how many suppliers I coordinate. After a free first meeting I prepare a personalised, transparent quote with no hidden costs. Supplier costs (venue, catering, flowers, photographer…) are paid separately.

With full planning I follow you from the very first idea: budget, venue, suppliers, styling, timeline and the day itself. Partial planning is for couples who have already chosen some suppliers and need help with the rest. On-the-day coordination means I take over in the final weeks, check every supplier and run the day so that you can simply enjoy it.

No: my fee covers planning, coordination and the time I dedicate to you. Venue, catering, floral design, photographer, music and the other suppliers are paid separately. I select them with you, compare quotes and make sure every cost is clear in advance.

It depends above all on the number of guests and on the venue: a reception in a restaurant costs very differently from an exclusive masseria booked for the whole weekend. Catering is usually priced per guest; on top of that come flowers and styling, photo and video, music, attire and extras such as transport and music rights. In the first meeting we build a realistic budget together and I help you decide where to invest and where to save.

Destination weddings and couples from abroad

I organise weddings and events in Lecce, throughout Salento and the rest of Puglia, as well as across Italy. I work with masserie, villas, historic residences and seaside venues all over the region.

Yes. I work with couples living abroad who wish to get married in Puglia, taking care of every stage remotely: from choosing the venue to the paperwork, right through to on-the-day coordination. I have even organised weddings remotely from the United States in just a few months.

Yes. We meet by video call (in English or French too), I send you proposals, quotes and video tours of the venues, and I take care of site visits and appointments here in Puglia. A trip before the wedding is welcome but not essential: one couple from the USA planned their whole wedding with me remotely in just 4 months.

Brindisi Airport (BDS) is about 40 km from Lecce, roughly a 35–40 minute drive. Bari Airport (BRI) is about two hours by car and offers more international connections. Lecce also has a railway station linked to Bari, Rome and Milan.

Yes, it is one of the things I love most. For a couple from Los Angeles I set up a real Salento town square — with permission from the mayor — with pizzica dancing and stalls of local specialities, so that their guests could live our land and not just see it. Welcome dinners, tastings and traditional music can all be part of your wedding weekend.

Ceremonies and legal matters

Yes, I am a celebrant of civil and symbolic ceremonies. I personally officiate in Italian, English and French; in Spanish, Russian and German with trusted partners. Symbolic rites include the sand ceremony, the light ceremony and tie-the-knot.

No. A symbolic ceremony has no legal effect, which is exactly why it can take place anywhere — on the beach, in a masseria, in a garden — and be fully personalised. Many couples sign the legal part at the town hall (in Italy or at home) and then celebrate the symbolic ceremony with their guests.

Only if the venue has been officially designated by the local town council as a place for civil weddings (a so-called separate registry office). Some masserie and historic residences in Puglia have this status; many beaches and private gardens do not. I check this with the town hall before you book, and where it is not possible we combine a short civil ceremony with a symbolic one at the venue.

Each spouse needs a declaration that there is no impediment to the marriage (the “nulla osta” or its equivalent issued by their country), a valid passport and, depending on the country, birth certificates with apostille and an official Italian translation. US citizens, for example, usually need an “Atto Notorio” and a “Dichiarazione Giurata”; British citizens a Certificate of No Impediment. Requirements change by nationality and town hall: I check them with you and with the town hall well in advance.

Yes — love is love. In Italy same-sex couples can enter a legally recognised civil union (Law 76/2016) at the town hall, and they can also celebrate a symbolic ceremony wherever they wish. I take care of both the organisation and the ceremony, with the same care as for every wedding.

Timing and organisation

Ideally 12 to 18 months before, especially for the most sought-after venues and for destination weddings. That said, I can also organise weddings at shorter notice, even in just a few months, with the same level of care.

The first meeting is all about getting to know you and listening to your idea of a wedding or event. From there we define the style, an indicative budget and the services, and I prepare a tailor-made proposal. You can book it by phone, WhatsApp or through the contact form.

May, June and September are the favourite months: warm days, cooler evenings and long sunsets. July and August are perfect for evening receptions by the sea but are hot and very busy, so venues and hotels must be booked well ahead. Late September and early October are lovely for more intimate weddings, with milder prices.

There is always a plan B. For outdoor ceremonies and receptions I choose venues with covered spaces or prepare a marquee in advance, and in the last week I monitor the forecast with the venue so that the decision is taken calmly and in time.

Yes. As an event planner I organise private parties, receptions, birthdays, anniversaries, gala evenings, shows and corporate meetings, taking care of venue, catering, music and floral styling.
