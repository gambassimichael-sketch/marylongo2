Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This notice explains how personal data of visitors to this website are processed, pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Data we process

  • Browsing data: the hosting provider automatically records technical data (IP address, browser, date and time of access) needed for the website to work and stay secure.
  • Data you provide: when you fill in the contact form (name, email, phone, message, chosen language) or write to us by email or WhatsApp.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Answering your requests and preparing quotes — pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Fulfilling legal, tax and accounting obligations if a contract follows (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Website security and proper functioning — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Showing external content such as Google Maps — only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Retention

Requests are kept for the time needed to handle them and in any case no longer than 24 months if no contract follows. Contract and tax data are kept for 10 years as required by law. Technical logs are kept by the hosting provider for a limited time.

5. Recipients

  • Netlify, Inc. (website hosting and contact form management), as data processor. Data may be transferred to the USA on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google, only if you choose to load the map on the Contact page.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), only if you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, as an independent controller.

Data are not sold or disseminated.

6. Providing data

Providing data through the form is optional, but without it we cannot answer your request. No automated decision-making takes place.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time (Arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to info@marylongoweddingplanner.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Cookies

For information on cookies and similar technologies, please see the Cookie Policy.
