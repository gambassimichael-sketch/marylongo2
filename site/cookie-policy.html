Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Cookies are small text files that websites save on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. This website does not use profiling or advertising cookies, nor statistics/analytics tools.

1. Technical tools (always active)

They are needed for the website to work and do not require consent.

Fonts are hosted on this website: no connection is made to Google Fonts.

2. External content (only with consent)

The Contact page can show a Google Maps map (Google Ireland Ltd). If you load it, Google may set its own cookies and collect your IP address. The map is loaded only after you accept “External content” or press “Load the map”. More information: Google privacy policy.

3. Links to social networks

The Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Matrimonio.com icons are simple links: they do not load anything and do not set cookies until you click them.

4. Managing your preferences

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via (also in the footer of every page). You can also delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings.

For everything else, see the Privacy Policy.
